## Maven Market BI Dashboard
**Project Overview** <br>
This project showcases an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed using Power BI on the Maven Market retail dataset.<br>
The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights through:<br>
⋗ Data Cleaning & Transformation (Power Query) <br>
⋗ Data Modeling (Star Schema) <br>
⋗ DAX Calculations & KPIs <br>
⋗ Interactive Data Visualization <br>
⋗ Business Performance Analysis <br>
This project demonstrates core competencies required for a Data Analyst role. <br><br>
**Business Problem** <br>
Retail businesses require visibility into: <br>
⋗ Revenue trends <br>
⋗ Profitability drivers <br>
⋗ Regional sales performance <br>
⋗ Product category contribution <br>
⋗ Customer purchasing behavior <br>
This dashboard enables data-driven decision-making by delivering real-time analytical insights. <br><br>
**Technical Skills & Tools Used** <br>
⋗ Power BI Desktop <br>
⋗ Power Query (ETL – Extract, Transform, Load) <br>
⋗ DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) <br>
⋗ Data Modeling (Fact & Dimension Tables) <br>
⋗ KPI Design & Business Metrics <br>
⋗ Data Visualization Best Practices <br><br>
**Key Dashboard Features** <br>
⋗ Revenue, Profit & Margin KPI Cards <br>
⋗ Month-over-Month & Year-over-Year Trend Analysis <br>
⋗ Region-wise Sales & Profit Breakdown <br>
⋗ Product Category & Sub-Category Performance <br>
⋗ Customer Segmentation Insights <br>
⋗ Dynamic Slicers & Drill-through Analysis <br><br>
**Key DAX Measures Implemented** <br>
⋗ Total Revenue <br>
⋗ Total Profit <br>
⋗ Profit Margin % <br>
⋗ Sales Growth % <br>
⋗ Year-to-Date (YTD) Sales <br>
⋗ Dynamic Filtering Metrics <br><br>
**Business Insights Generated** <br>
⋗ Identified high-revenue product categories driving profitability <br>
⋗ Detected underperforming regions requiring strategic focus <br>
⋗ Analyzed seasonal sales trends <br>
⋗ Evaluated customer contribution to total revenue <br>
⋗ Measured profit margins across segments <br><br>
**How to Run** <br>
Download the .pbix file <br>
Open in Power BI Desktop <br>
Refresh data source (if required) <br>
Interact with filters, slicers, and drill-down visuals <br><br>
